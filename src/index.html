Multigrain roti sakht to nahi banti?

Nahi, agar aata neem garam paani se gundh kar 20 minute rakha jaye. Har pack ke peeche tareeqa likha hai.

Nahi. Khosha khana hai, dawa nahi. Hamare atta mein fibre wale desi anaaj hain. Apni diet ke baare mein apne doctor se mashwara karein.
